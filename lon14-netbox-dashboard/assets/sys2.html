How sys2 is wired

P1
Two separate planes. Plane A = BEL1–128 + BES1–36, plane B = BEL129–256 + BES37–72. No cable joins the planes; every host has one port in each plane per rail.
L1
Leaf uplinks are swp47s0 … swp64s1 (36 split ports, one per spine of its plane). Leaf j uplink u (0–35) → spine 36p+36−u; on the spine it lands on port index j−1−128p (swp1s0, swp1s1, swp2s0 …). Each spine has 128 leaf ports.
G1
Hosts have 8 backend ports swprsp: NIC r = rail 1–4, split p = plane A (s0) or B (s1). A pod is 144 hosts (gpu1–144, 145–288 …) on 16 leaves per plane, in four rail blocks of four. SU k (36 hosts) takes the k-th leaf of each rail block: gpu1–36 → BEL1 / 5 / 9 / 13 (A) and BEL129 / 133 / 137 / 141 (B). Host i of an SU uses leaf port swp(⌊i/2⌋+1)s(i mod 2) on all eight leaves.
